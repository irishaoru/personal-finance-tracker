How we calculate these patterns

All summaries use posted records in the selected period and currency. Positive amounts are outflows; negative amounts are inflows. Transfers and loan payments may be included in outflows. Small purchases are positive transactions under 20 currency units. Weekday totals describe the data, not your future behavior. Pending records appear in the list but do not affect summaries.
